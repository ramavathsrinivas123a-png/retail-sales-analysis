# Retail Sales Analysis — Business Insights

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data using Excel and SQL to identify sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and product category performance.

## Dataset
- Total records: 1,200
- Time period: 2025
- Product categories: Electronics, Accessories, Furniture, Stationery
- Regions: South, Central, North, East, West

## 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total sales: 12,127,449.50
- Total profit: 1,768,067.22
- Units sold: 1,792
- Total records: 1,200
- Average sales per record: 10,106.21
- Overall profit margin: 14.62%

## 2. Category Analysis
- Electronics generated the highest sales: 8,938,740.
- Furniture generated 2,703,200 in sales.
- Accessories generated 456,755 in sales.
- Stationery generated the lowest sales: 28,774.50.

### Business Insights
- Electronics is the leading revenue-generating category.
- Furniture is the second-largest category by sales.
- Accessories and Stationery contribute considerably less sales compared with Electronics and Furniture.

## 3. Regional Analysis
- West recorded the highest sales: 3,068,568.50 and highest profit: 440,951.28.
- South generated 2,397,053 in sales.
- North generated 2,296,066.50 in sales.
- Central generated 2,271,870 in sales.
- East recorded the lowest sales: 2,093,921.50.

### Business Insights
- West is the strongest-performing region in sales and profit.
- East is the lowest-performing region in sales.
- Regional performance varies, so business strategies could be reviewed for lower-performing regions.

## 4. Profitability Analysis
- Total profit: 1,768,067.22.
- Overall profit margin: 14.62%.
- Electronics generated the highest category profit: 1,248,571.11.
- West generated the highest regional profit: 440,951.28.

### Business Insights
- Electronics is the largest contributor to both sales and profit.
- West is the most profitable region in the analysis.
- Sales and profit should be considered together when evaluating performance.

## 5. Recommendations
1. Maintain inventory availability for Electronics, the leading category.
2. Investigate the lower sales performance in the East region.
3. Review the performance of Accessories and Stationery for potential growth opportunities.
4. Monitor monthly sales trends for changes over time.
5. Compare regional sales and profitability to support business decisions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- SQL (SQLite)
- GitHub

## Conclusion
The analysis identified Electronics as the leading product category and West as the strongest-performing region. Excel and SQL were used to analyze sales, profit, units sold, and transaction records to produce useful business insights.
