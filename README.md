# Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to evaluate business performance, identify profitable product categories, compare regional performance, and explore monthly sales trends.

The project uses Microsoft Excel and SQL (SQLite) to clean, summarize, analyze, and interpret sales data.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Evaluate regional sales and profitability.
- Analyze monthly sales trends.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Analyze payment methods.
- Derive actionable business insights.

## Dataset
- **Records:** 1,200
- **Time period:** 2025
- **Categories:** Electronics, Accessories, Furniture, Stationery
- **Regions:** South, Central, North, East, West
- **Columns:** 12

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- SQL (SQLite)
- GitHub

## Project Workflow
1. Data inspection and validation
2. Data quality checks
3. KPI calculation
4. Regional and category analysis
5. PivotTables and charts in Excel
6. SQL-based analysis
7. Business insights and recommendations

## Key Findings

### Product Category
- Electronics generated the highest sales: 8,938,740.
- Furniture generated 2,703,200 in sales.
- Accessories generated 456,755 in sales.
- Stationery generated 28,774.50 in sales.

### Regional Performance
- West had the highest sales: 3,068,568.50.
- West had the highest regional profit: 440,951.28.
- East recorded the lowest regional sales: 2,093,921.50.

## SQL Analysis
SQL queries were used to:
- Filter and sort records
- Apply multiple conditions
- Analyze sales using `GROUP BY`
- Calculate totals using `SUM`, `COUNT`, and `AVG`
- Analyze monthly sales
- Identify top 10 sales records
- Analyze payment methods
- Identify top 10 products by total sales

## Business Recommendations
- Maintain availability of products in the leading Electronics category.
- Investigate the comparatively lower sales in the East region.
- Review sales performance of Accessories and Stationery.
- Monitor monthly trends and regional performance.
- Evaluate profitability alongside revenue when making business decisions.
